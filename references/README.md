# Style references from Ansh (Nov 17 2026)

Ansh sent these two reels as the models for the @shipwithpip what-if and explainer reels. Contact sheets are in this folder; the source videos are Instagram exports (not stored here).

## 1. The nail reel (what-if model) `nail_hammer_sheet.png`
41 s, 3D cartoon, bright sky-blue background, one construction worker, a hammer, a nail, a plank.
Structure: an **escalation chain where each step makes one more thing indestructible**, so the failure moves up the chain:
1. Right as you swing, the wood becomes indestructible: the nail bends.
2. The nail was also indestructible: the hammer head flies right off.
3. The hammer is indestructible too: the force goes into your arm (arm breaks, x-ray style shot).
4. Your body is indestructible: you swing the hammer down at the speed of light.
5. Payoff: instead of driving the nail, all that energy is released: a blinding flash, a shockwave flattening the town around it.
6. Ending: the town is gone, the nail is still there, and you.
What makes it work: an everyday object everyone has held, one small change per beat, every beat ends on a visible consequence, the stakes jump from a nail to a town, big centred word-by-word captions, a speed number on screen at the climax.
Note: the reel shows "292,950 mp/s" for light speed, which is wrong. Ours stay honest: real values, real physics, computed in `calc`.

## 2. The chip reel (cinematic how-it-works explainer model) `chip_explainer_sheet.png`
141 s, dark navy and warm gold glow, cinematic 3D zoom. Sand grown into a crystal, a chip the size of a fingernail with "100,000,000,000" over it, then zoom in: one switch (voltage on = 1), two switches in a row = a gate (OR), gates add and subtract (8 - 3 = 5), a memorized bit, memory, a program as 1s and 0s, decode and execute, "let's slow down time" with a stopwatch (one instruction at human speed takes "Day 3" vs "0:02"), ends wide on a phone and a sunset.
What makes it work: start from a real object in your hand, zoom one level at a time, each level is one idea with one big word or number on screen, a time-scale twist near the end, highlighted keywords in captions.
